Ati akka mana irraa fagoo deemte akka ta'e beekteetta? Akkam bulteessan, isin hundi kan Afaan Oromoo dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu jirtan, ani gammade isin as jirtan.

Ilmi xiqqoon qooda isaa gaafate, biyya fagoo deeme, qarshii hundumaa isaatuu fixise. Yeroo beelli dhufte, inni beelee akka nyaata booyyee gaafate. Dhuma irratti inni yaadate: "Mana abbaa kootti hojjettoonniyyuu quufan. Ani gara deebi'a." Yeroo inni amma fagoo jiru, abbaan isaa arganii, fuudhee, isaa hammachuu, isaa dhungate. "Ilma kooti du'ee, amma deebi'ee jira; baduu, amma argame jira." Sana booda isaan ayyaana godhan.

Waaqni akka kana simachuun maal atiif sitti dhagahama? Maal ati gara deebi'uu qabda?

Yoo kana Afaan Oromootiin dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu addunyaa kanaa — yeroo muraasa fudhattanii Jonathan nagaa gaafachuu dandeessu?

*Jechoota Macaafa Qulqulluu kan as keessa jiran kana bakka interneetaa kanaaf hiikaman.*
