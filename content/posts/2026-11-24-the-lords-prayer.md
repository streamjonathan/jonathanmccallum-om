Akka kadhachuu fedhu garuu maal jedhu hin beekne maal fakkaata? Akkam bulteessan, isin hundi kan Afaan Oromoo dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu jirtan, ani gammade isin as jirtan.

Barattootni Yesuus akka isaan kadhachuu barsiisu gaafatan. Inni, "Yeroo ati kadhattu, akkas jedhi: Abbaa keenya samii keessa jiru, maqaan kee haa qulqulla'u. Mootummaan kee haa dhufu, fedhiin kee lafa irratti haa raawwatamu, akka samii keessatti. Har'a nyaata keenya guyyaa guyyaa nuuf kenni. Liqaa keenya nuuf dhiifi, akka nu iyyuu kan nu liqaa qaban dhiifnu. Qoramsa keessa nu hin geessin, hammaa irraa nu oolchi" jedhe. Kadhanni kun akka nu Waaqa Abbaa akka dubbachuu dandeenyu, guyyaa hundumaa waan nu barbaadnu gaafachuu dandeenyu barsiisa.

Jechuun kadhata kana keessatti kamtu atiif caala dubbata? Ati har'a maal gaafachuu fedha?

Yoo kana Afaan Oromootiin dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu addunyaa kanaa — yeroo muraasa fudhattanii Jonathan nagaa gaafachuu dandeessu?

*Jechoota Macaafa Qulqulluu kan as keessa jiran kana bakka interneetaa kanaaf hiikaman.*
