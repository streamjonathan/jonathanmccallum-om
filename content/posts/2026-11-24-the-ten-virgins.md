Eeggachuun rakkisaadha, qophaa'uun caala rakkisaadha. Akkam bulteessan, isin hundi kan Afaan Oromoo dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu jirtan, ani gammade isin as jirtan.

Shamarran kudhan ibaaffa qabdanii furii eeggan. Shan ogeessan zayita dheedduu qabdan, shan gowwan hin qabne. Furii yeroo ture, hundumti raftee. Halkan gidduu, "Furii dhufteera!" jedhame. Gowwan zayitii isaanii dhumnaan ba'e, bitachuuf ba'an. Yeroo isaan deebi'an, balballi cufameera. Yesuus, "Eeggadhaa, guyyaa fi sa'aa hin beektan" jedhe.

Ati maal eegaa? Qophaa'uun atiif maal jechuu dha?

Yoo kana Afaan Oromootiin dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu addunyaa kanaa — yeroo muraasa fudhattanii Jonathan nagaa gaafachuu dandeessu?

*Jechoota Macaafa Qulqulluu kan as keessa jiran kana bakka interneetaa kanaaf hiikaman.*
