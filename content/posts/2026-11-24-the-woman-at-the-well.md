Ati akka dheebotu beekta — bishaan qofa hin taane. Akkam bulteessan, isin hundi kan Afaan Oromoo dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu jirtan, ani gammade isin as jirtan.

Yesuus guyyaa keessaa bishaan boolla Samariyaa irratti taa'ee. Dubartiin tokkoon bishaan waraabachuuf dhufte. Yesuus, "Bishaan akka ani dhugdu naaf kenni" jedhe. Isheen dinqisiifte — Yihudoonni Samariyaa waliin hin dubbatan. Yesuus, "Ati kennaa Waaqaa yoo beekta, ati naa gaafatta, ani bishaan jireenyaa si kenna ture. Kan bishaan kana dhugu deebi'ee dheebota, garuu kan bishaan ani kennu dhugu bara baraan hin dheebotu" jedhe. Inni jireenya isheetii, waan isheen godhe hundumaa irratti dubbate. Isheen godoo isheetti dhiiftee, magaalaa deemtee, namootatti, "Kottaa, namicha waan ani godhe hundumaa naa hime argadhaa. Inni Kiristoos dha?" jedhe.

Ati amma maal dheebota? Ati eenyuuf Yesuus irratti himta?

Yoo kana Afaan Oromootiin dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu addunyaa kanaa — yeroo muraasa fudhattanii Jonathan nagaa gaafachuu dandeessu?

*Jechoota Macaafa Qulqulluu kan as keessa jiran kana bakka interneetaa kanaaf hiikaman.*
