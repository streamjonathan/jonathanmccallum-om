Jireenyi kee maal irra dhaabatte murteessaadha. Akkam bulteessan, isin hundi kan Afaan Oromoo dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu jirtan, ani gammade isin as jirtan.

Yesuus, "Kan dubbii koo dhaga'ee, raawwate, namicha ogeessaa mana dhagaa irra ijaare fakkaata" jedhe. Roobni roobe, bishaanichi dhufte, bubbeen mana rukute — garuu inni hin jigu, dhagaa irra dhaabameef. "Kan dubbii koo dhaga'ee, hin raawwatne, namicha gowwaa mana caccabaa irra ijaare fakkaata. Roobni, bishaanichi, bubbeen dhufte, mani jige, jigummaan isaa guddaa ta'e."

Jireenyi kee maal irra dhaabatte? Bubbeen yeroo dhufte maal ta'a?

Yoo kana Afaan Oromootiin dubbiftan — bakka kamittuu addunyaa kanaa — yeroo muraasa fudhattanii Jonathan nagaa gaafachuu dandeessu?

*Jechoota Macaafa Qulqulluu kan as keessa jiran kana bakka interneetaa kanaaf hiikaman.*
